Asian Paints — What 10 Years of Data Tells Us

A full 10-year financial analysis of Asian Paints, built from raw balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow data — with ratio analysis, trend spotting, and a competitor comparison against Berger Paints and Kansai Nerolac.

Quick Takeaways
  • Asian Paints consistently generated strong profitability, with ROE reaching 29.7% in FY 2024 — among the highest in its sector.
  • Revenue grew consistently over the decade, though profit growth was not always as smooth, recovering to a 15.7% net margin by FY 2024.
  • Investing activity grew sharply, from ₹465 crore in FY 2015 to ₹2,548 crore in FY 2024, funded by a manageable rise in borrowings.
  • Asian Paints held a clear competitive edge over Berger Paints and Kansai Nerolac on ROE, margins, and debtor days.
  • The open question: can these returns hold as the company keeps expanding into new categories and geographies?
